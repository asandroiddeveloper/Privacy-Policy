# Privacy Policy for AS Weather

**Developer:** AS (ASDROID)

**Effective Date:** September 18, 2026

**Repository:** [github.com/asandroiddeveloper/AS-Weather](https://github.com/asandroiddeveloper/AS-Weather)

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **AS Weather**. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a transparent experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application.

## 2. Permissions & Data Usage
AS Weather requires certain permissions to function correctly:

*   **Location Access (Precise/Coarse):** Used to fetch real-time weather data for your current area and to calculate the exact direction of the Qibla. Coordinates are sent to third-party APIs (OpenWeatherMap and Nominatim) to fulfill these requests but are **not** stored by the developer.
*   **Camera Access:** Used exclusively for the **Augmented Reality (AR) Qibla Finder**. The camera feed is processed locally on your device to anchor the Kaaba icon in your surroundings. No images or videos are recorded, stored, or uploaded.
*   **Notifications:** Used to send periodic weather updates and safety alerts. Notifications are scheduled and generated locally on your device — we do not use a remote push server to send them. You can disable this at any time in the app settings.

## 3. Third-Party Services
To provide our services, we interact with the following providers:

*   **OpenWeatherMap:** Provides current weather, forecasts, and air quality data.
*   **Nominatim (OpenStreetMap):** Provides geocoding to resolve coordinates into human-readable city names.
*   **Firebase Crashlytics (Planned):** We may integrate Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports and diagnostic logs (device model, OS version, stack traces). This data is anonymized and does not include personal information.
*   **Advertising Platforms (Planned):** We may integrate advertising services in future updates to support the app's development. These platforms may collect device identifiers to serve relevant ads.

*Note: While some services like Crashlytics or Ads are not currently integrated, this policy accounts for their potential inclusion in future updates. This policy will be updated whenever new integrations are added.*

## 4. Data Collection & Security
*   **No PII:** We do **not** collect, store, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
*   **Offline First:** Most settings and preferences are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore/Room.

## 5. Policy Updates
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. Significant changes will be highlighted within the app's Settings menu.
